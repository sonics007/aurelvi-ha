WHEN PLUS LAUNCHES

A simple path to activation.

01

Choose your panel

Enter the device ID shown in the Aurelvi app. A Plus licence is issued for one panel.

02

Pay securely with Stripe

At launch, payment will take place on Stripe Checkout. This site will not collect card numbers.

03

Apply your signed licence

A server-issued token will confirm your panel entitlement. Payment is verified on the server before issuing it.

Required when checkout is enabled. Do not enter your MQTT password or HA access token.

No change to your existing controls

Current releases remain fully usable. Premium packs, licence application in HA/APK, payment processing and recurring billing must be connected and tested before commercial launch. A licence purchased later will apply to its supported future release, not retroactively lock an older build. Final tax treatment and checkout details will be shown before payment.
