SMALL PANEL. BIG POSSIBILITIES.

Designed around your day.

One familiar place for the things you use most. Aurelvi connects your NSPanel Pro to your Home Assistant entities.

Fans & lights

Up to six speeds, custom presets, direction, brightness, colour temperature and timed shutoff. Controls depend on the capabilities of your HA entity.

A layout for every room

Visual grids, switches, dimmers, covers and readouts. Build room drafts controls from a selected HA area; review them before saving.

A gentler morning

A dedicated alarm editor, four original alarm tone choices, volume and optional gradual fade-in in APK 0.3.7.

Your own look

80 original palettes and procedural materials, five wallpapers, your own image upload, separate clock and screensaver styling.

Local by design

MQTT carries controls and confirmed states. The panel does not need a cloud account for local control. Internet is needed for downloads and online services.

Notifications & more

HA notifications with actions, a weather page, camera/MJPEG pages with bound PTZ actions, and a native media player integration.

Setup without a terminal

Connect over network ADB, install the latest official APK, set Aurelvi as Home and manage the device from Studio. Accept the first ADB prompt on the panel.

A quieter evening

Screensaver timing, night styling, brightness settings and optional proximity/motion behaviour. Sensor functions require sensor support from Android.

A note on compatibility

Aurelvi is a community project. Native sensors, audio and launcher commands vary by panel firmware. Sendspin remains experimental. A supported HA entity is required for each control; test a new release on your own device.

Intercom, with a clear connection.

APK 0.3.11 adds a SIP/WebRTC intercom preview. Choose one of up to 12 phone contacts, answer incoming calls explicitly, mute or hang up. A compatible SIP/WSS service and phone app are required. Locked-screen ringing needs provider push support; Aurelvi does not supply that service. Real-device calling still requires verification.
