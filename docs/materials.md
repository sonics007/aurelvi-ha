# Digital materials

Eight 640 × 640 WebP textures are bundled in `custom_components/aurelvi/frontend/materials` and `website/assets/materials`. The Android assets use the same files. They depict aluminium, titanium, carbon fibre, oak, walnut, white marble, black marble and leather. These are AI-generated photorealistic digital surfaces, not photographs or claims about the panel's physical housing.

Created using the built-in image generation tool, as two square 2 × 2 atlases, then separated and encoded for local use. No runtime image service or external texture URL is used. Skin definitions choose `texture` independently from control geometry, with an optional wooden trim. The 80 skin IDs remain stable.

## Prompt set

1. One square 2 × 2 atlas of evenly lit, orthographic macro material scans: top-left genuine satin silver brushed aluminium with very fine horizontal hairlines; top-right natural European light oak with organic long vertical grain and pores; bottom-left dark American walnut with subtle natural cathedral grain; bottom-right polished ivory Carrara marble with thin branching grey mineral veins. Equal quadrants edge-to-edge, no gaps, objects, labels, logos, borders or perspective; tactile photorealism suitable behind readable wall controls.
2. One square 2 × 2 atlas: top-left black 2 × 2 twill carbon fibre with restrained satin resin and individual filaments; top-right soft grey satin titanium with fine horizontal brushed hairlines; bottom-left charcoal fine-grained leather with tiny organic pores and no stitching; bottom-right polished black Nero Marquina marble with sparse fine white branching veins. Orthographic macro scans, even diffuse illumination, continuous texture in each quadrant, no text, objects, decorative borders or artificial gradients.
